# Game terms

The game's concepts as a match document and the rules documents name them.
`game` marks Chinese the game's own text uses; `ours` marks a term the game
does not show in Chinese, chosen here.

## Match

| English | 中文 | Source | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| match | 对局 | ours | one game between two sides, from the opening to the end, played over rounds |
| round | 回合 | game | one turn of a match: both sides buy and place, then a fight is fought |
| this round | 本回合 | game | an effect that lasts until the round it was bought in ends |
| side | 阵营 | ours | one of the two players, `blue` or `red` |
| opening | 开局 | ours | what a side holds before round 1: its advance team or specialist |
| advance team | 先遣队 | ours | an opening that hands a side units |
| specialist | 专家 | game | an opening, or a reinforcement card, that hands a side an officer |
| map | 地图 | ours | the board a match is fought on, named by its ID |
| territory | 领地 | ours | a region of a map a side may place in, opening in a given round |
| reactor core | 反应堆核心 | ours | a side's life; a round's loser loses some, and a side at none has lost |

## Economy

| English | 中文 | Source | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| supply | 补给 | game | the currency a side spends in the shop |
| shop | 商店 | game | where a side buys units and cards each round |
| unlock | 解锁 | game | buying the right to buy a unit |
| upgrade | 升级 | game | raising a formation's level |
| level | 等级 | game | a formation's rank, which multiplies its life and damage |
| experience | 经验 | game | what a formation earns in a fight towards its next level |
| reinforcement card | 增援卡 | ours | a card the shop offers between rounds: units, an officer, equipment or a commander skill |
| blueprint | 蓝图 | game | a purchase that puts a commander skill or an officer within reach for the match |
| sell | 出售 | ours | giving a formation back for part of its price |

## Forces

| English | 中文 | Source | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| unit | 单位 | game | a kind of mech, and the type a formation is made of |
| formation | 编队 | ours | the units bought as one card and placed as one, which level and research together |
| squad | 小队 | game | one member of a formation |
| technology | 科技 | game | an upgrade a formation researches, which changes its unit for the match |
| officer | 军官 | ours | a side-wide bonus a card or a specialist grants |
| equipment | 装备 | game | an item a formation wears, at most one per formation |
| commander skill | 战场技能 | game | a skill a side releases on the board; the game's English calls it a Battlefield Power |
| energy tower | 能量塔 | game | a building whose skill a side may use, and whose loss weakens its side |
| energy tower skill | 能量塔技能 | ours | the skill a side picks for its energy tower |
| contraption | 装置 | ours | a device a side places: a shield, a missile or an interceptor |
| construction | 建筑 | ours | a building a side places, such as a wall or a turret |

## Fight

| English | 中文 | Source | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fight | 战斗 | ours | the part of a round where the board plays itself out |
| tick | 帧 | ours | one step of a fight's simulation |
| deploy | 部署 | game | placing a formation on the board before a fight |
| damage | 伤害 | game | what a hit takes off a target's life |
| shield | 护盾 | game | a layer that takes damage before life does |
| range | 射程 | game | how far a unit attacks |
| buff | 增益 | ours | an effect a source puts on a unit for a while, whether it helps or harms |
