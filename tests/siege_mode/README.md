# Siege mode

When a siege-mode technology digs its unit in, what the trench gives it,
and what takes it away.
[`technology_effects.md`](../../docs/rules/technology_effects.md#siege-mode-technologies)
is the rule. Each fight puts Field Entrenchment on blue's Sabertooth or
Typhoons against red Arclights close by and far off, and then puts red's wall
in range, an Electromagnetic Impact on it, a Hacker after it, ends the fight
with it dug in, brings it in travelling from a flank, or kills it in its
trench.

A recording holds the trench in each dug-in unit's `motion_state`
(`stopped`), its life and maximum, and its main skill's range.
