# Burrow

When a burrowing technology burrows its unit and brings it up, what its
burrow takes off the hits on it, and what happens to it as the unit dies or
the fight ends.
[`technology_effects.md`](../../docs/rules/technology_effects.md#burrowing-technologies)
states the rule. Each fight is its layout fought once in the game, read back
as a fight; the burrow is the technology's buff in the units' state, and its
writing and removal are `buff_applied` and `buff_removed` events.
