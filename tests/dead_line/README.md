# Dead line

What a dead-line technology does to what its unit's main skill hits: the life
at or under which a hit destroys its target whole, by the unit's level, and
what the destruction is recorded as.
[`technology_effects.md`](../../docs/rules/technology_effects.md#dead-line-technologies)
is the rule. Each fight puts Culling Rounds on a blue Mustang squad, at level
one against Crawlers and at level three against Marksmen.
