# Reactive armor

What a reactive armor technology takes off the hits on its unit, for how
many hits, and what a buff that disables technology does to it.
[`technology_effects.md`](../../docs/rules/technology_effects.md#reactive-armor-technologies)
states the rule. Each fight is its layout fought once in the game, read back
as a fight; the hits on the armored unit are its `damage` events.
