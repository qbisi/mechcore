# Wreckage

How a wreckage-recovery technology heals its unit as an enemy it struck
dies: who heals, by how much, and what a disable changes.
[`technology_effects.md`](../../docs/rules/technology_effects.md#wreckage-recovery-technologies)
is the rule. Each fight puts Wreckage Recycling on blue's Rhinos or Abysses
against Marksmen, Mustangs, a Vulcan and a far Arclight, one of them under an
Electromagnetic Impact.

A recording holds each heal as a `healing` event on the unit, after the
`unit_died` it follows.
