# Stealth

What a stealth technology does to its unit once it is hurt: when it goes into
stealth and comes out, what strikes it and what finds it meanwhile, and what a
buff that disables technology does to it.
[`technology_effects.md`](../../docs/rules/technology_effects.md#stealth-technologies)
is the rule. Each fight puts Emergency Armor on a blue Vortex that red's
Marksmen shoot, two of them with an Electromagnetic Impact of red's landing
on the Vortex before it is hurt or while it is in stealth.

A recording holds the Vortex's `visibility` and `targetable` every tick, and
the Marksmen's locks and attack targets.
