# Damage share

What a damage-share technology does: which of its units link into a group,
and how a hit on one member is shared among them all.
[`technology_effects.md`](../../docs/rules/technology_effects.md#damage-share-technologies)
is the rule. Each fight puts Damage Sharing on blue's Sledgehammers or Steel
Balls, side by side or far apart, and strikes them with shots, blows and
beams.

A recording holds a `damage` event on every member for each shared hit, in
the group's order.
