# Splash

How a correction widens a skill's splash, and what a skill with no splash of
its own does once it has one. [`combat.md`](../../docs/rules/combat.md#damage-and-death)
is the rule. Each fight puts a splash value on one unit shooting a Crawler
formation, which a splash shows: an item on a unit with no splash and on one
with its own, a technology, and two officers. The item and technology fights'
controls are the Crawler fights of [`../marksman/`](../marksman/) and
[`../arclight/`](../arclight/).
