# Energy Tower skills

What the Energy Tower's two fight skills, Enhanced Range and High Mobility,
write onto the side that activated them, and what they leave alone.
[`energy_tower_skills.md`](../../docs/rules/energy_tower_skills.md) is the rule.
Each fight here puts the skills on blue only, so red's units of the same type
are the control, except in `turret-untouched.yaml`, whose control is
[`../turret/fights/anti-armor-arclights.yaml`](../turret/fights/anti-armor-arclights.yaml).
