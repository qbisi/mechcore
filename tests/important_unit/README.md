# Important units

What a side does when its last important unit dies: who dies with it, when,
and credited to whom.
[`equipment_effects.md`](../../docs/rules/equipment_effects.md#an-important-unit)
is the rule. Each fight puts Dominion Core on blue and changes one thing from
`core-falls.yaml`, which its comment names: a second core, a formation of
many, a shield on the unit left standing, or a melee kill.
