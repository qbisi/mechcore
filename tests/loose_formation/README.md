# Loose formation

How far apart a loose-formation technology keeps its unit's agents as they
walk, when they close up again, and what a buff that disables technology
does to it.
[`technology_effects.md`](../../docs/rules/technology_effects.md#loose-formation-technologies)
states the rule. Each fight is its layout fought once in the game, read back
as a fight; the agents' spacing shows in the units' positions.
